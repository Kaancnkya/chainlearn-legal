The app supports three languages (English, Spanish, Turkish) as both UI languages and learning languages.

## 5. AI-Generated Content Disclaimer

ChainLearn uses Large Language Models (Claude, by Anthropic) to generate example sentences, definitions, and to perform optical character recognition (Book Scan). LLM output may occasionally be:

- **Inaccurate** — definitions may oversimplify, sentences may sound slightly off in idiomatic register.
- **Biased** — LLMs can reflect biases present in their training data.
- **Unexpected** — generated content is not deterministic and may produce different results for the same input.

We make best-effort use of fallback layers (cached real-world examples from Tatoeba and YouTube precede LLM generation), but we do not guarantee accuracy. The app is a learning aid, not an authoritative dictionary or translator.
