## Common questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the App Store app → tap your profile → Subscriptions → ChainLearn → Cancel Subscription. Apple manages all subscription billing.

### How do I delete my account?
Open ChainLearn → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your ChainLearn data from our servers.

### What languages does ChainLearn support?
You can learn English, Spanish, or Turkish. The app interface is also available in English, Turkish, and Spanish.

### Where is my data stored?
Your vocabulary chain is stored in our Supabase backend and synced to your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
